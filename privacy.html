Privacy Policy

Canadian Loonie Coin Toss (Version 5), by Mark Pieczonka. Effective 13 October 2026.

What this app collects

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for or use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or any other personal information.

How the app works

The Heads or Tails result is chosen randomly on your own device and is never stored or sent anywhere. The app saves its own files (images, sound and page) on your device so that it can work offline.

Third parties

The first time the app loads, it fetches its text font (Montserrat) from Google Fonts, and the app is delivered from GitHub Pages. Like any website, those services may receive your IP address and device/browser details as part of the request. This is governed by Google's and GitHub's own privacy policies. The app itself does not receive or keep that information.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
